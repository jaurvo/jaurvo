Jaurvo

01, About

A full team. One studio.

Jaurvo is a web & design studio headquartered in Hitech City, Hyderabad, India, founded by Jauhar Ayyub, a Software Engineer. Behind every project stands a complete team, design, development, content, data and AI, building websites, brands and invitation experiences for clients across the world.

Because the whole team works under one roof, your project never waits on an outside freelancer. One studio confirms your scope and fixed price in writing, builds it, and answers for it after launch.

The team works in English, Hindi and Urdu daily, and builds bilingual English / Arabic websites for Gulf clients, so every client gets a website that feels made for them.
