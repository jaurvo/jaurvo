Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Applies to this website (jaurvo.vercel.app)

The short version

This website is a static brochure site. It has no accounts, no database and no analytics or advertising trackers. We only ever receive the information you choose to send us yourself, through the enquiry form or email, and we use it only to answer you, quote your project and deliver your work. We never sell your data, and we never share it with marketers.

What you send us, and why

When you contact Jaurvo, you do it through the enquiry form on our Contact page or by email. In that conversation you may share:

  • Your name and your business's name
  • Your phone number or email address
  • Details about the project you want, for example a menu, photos, a price list or invitation-card details

We use these details for one purpose only: to respond to your enquiry, prepare your quote, build and deliver your project, and provide support for it afterwards. Enquiries sent through the form are stored in our private project database so we do not lose track of your request. Messages you send by email are also subject to your email provider's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

What this website itself collects

Nothing about you is tracked, profiled or sent to advertisers by this website. There are no sign-ups and no tracking cookies. The only things stored on your own device are your preferences, the light or dark theme and your display currency, kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your choices. They never leave your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Like most websites, this site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to download them. No Jaurvo account or data is involved in that request.

Photos and material you share for a project

Photos, menus, logos and other material you send us for your project are used only to build that project. Material that appears on your finished, live website (for example your restaurant's photos) is public by design, that is the point of a website. Private source files you share are not published anywhere else by us.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what personal information of yours we hold in our email or enquiry records, and ask us to delete it. Write to {{ email }} and we will act on your request.

Changes to this policy

If this website ever adds new features that handle your information differently, this policy will be updated first to explain exactly what changes, before the change goes live.
